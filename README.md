# Privacy Policy — York Clock

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

York Clock ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how York Clock handles information when you use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

York Clock is designed to provide clock, stopwatch, and alarm functionality without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

**We do not directly collect, store, or sell personal information from users.**

The App does not require:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Password
* Account registration
* Contacts
* Photos or videos
* Location information
* Personal files
* Payment information

## 2. Clock, Stopwatch and Alarm

York Clock provides the following features:

* Digital clock functionality
* Stopwatch functionality
* Alarm functionality

The App may use the device's local system functionality to operate these features. Alarm settings and related information may be stored locally on your device as necessary for the App to provide its functionality.

We do not transmit your clock, stopwatch, or alarm information to our servers.

## 3. Advertising — Google AdMob

York Clock uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

AdMob is provided by Google and may collect or process certain information automatically, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interaction information, and other technical information, depending on your device settings, applicable laws, and Google's advertising configuration.

This information may be used by Google to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

York Clock does not directly access or control the information collected by Google AdMob.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policies and advertising documentation.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party service:

**Google AdMob** — Advertising service

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices through Google's official privacy resources.

## 5. Data Storage

York Clock does not operate a backend server for collecting or storing users' personal information.

Information required for the basic operation of the App, such as alarm settings, may remain locally on your device.

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

## 6. Children's Privacy

York Clock does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the App does not require users to create an account or submit personal information, users can use the basic functionality without providing personal details.

However, third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob may process information according to their own policies and applicable regulations.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to ensure that York Clock operates securely. Since we do not maintain a database of users' personal information, we do not maintain a server-side personal information database for York Clock.

## 8. Your Choices

You can control certain advertising and privacy-related settings through your Android device and Google account settings.

You may also uninstall York Clock at any time to stop using the application.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or our services.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be made available through the appropriate location associated with York Clock.
